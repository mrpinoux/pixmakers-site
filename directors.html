Directors

We are a group of individuals, a collective of video directors, a collaboration of artists with a shared vision, a shared passion. We are Pixmakers.

Founded by Mr Pinoux, on the concept that we are born from a generation of technology users and thus pixel manipulators, Pixmakers deals in video and image. Everything is connected, for us, through computers and pixels.

Studios in Los Angeles & Paris.
