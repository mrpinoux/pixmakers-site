Arilès De Tizi

Director / Painter / Photographer

Arilès De Tizi is a self-taught creative force, originally from Paris. A multidisciplinary artist, he effortlessly navigates the worlds of filmmaking, painting, photography and graphic design.

At 29, he has established himself as a dynamic globetrotter, known for his boundless creativity and hands-on approach — whether through scratching techniques or manipulating digital tools. From New York to Jakarta, and from Kiev to Bangkok, his projects span the globe, blending cultural influences.

He continues to collaborate with diverse artists, constantly expanding his creative repertoire and redefining boundaries.

Arilès De Tizi est un autodidacte, originaire de Paris. Artiste complet, il passe du film à la peinture, de la photographie au graphisme.

À 29 ans, il s’est imposé comme un globe-trotteur infatigable, de New York à Bangkok, avec un œil reconnaissable entre tous.

Il continue de collaborer avec des artistes très différents, élargissant sans cesse le champ de ce qu’il fabrique.

Disciplines
Film, painting, photography, graphic design
Cities
