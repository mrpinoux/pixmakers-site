Mr. Pinoux

Founder / Director

A self-taught filmmaker, originally from Paris where he founded Pixmakers. He moved to NYC, then Los Angeles in 2010. He shot his first music video for Neochrome Paris in 1998.

His background in graphic design shows in strong imagery that combines graphical elements, colour and shapes. Progressive imagery driven by attitude, youthfulness and music, with frenetic editing and animation techniques.

He has worked and collaborated with many artists — Seth Gueko, Loko, Chris Taylor, Syclone, Designers — as well as brands: Rigards, Baramix, Folie Douce, Simard Bilodeau Galerie.
